FAQ

What counts as a "live link"?

A placement we re-visit on the directory page and find pointing at your URL — checked by URL match, encoded variants and hostname. Until it passes that check, it isn't billed.

What if a link never goes live?

Automatic retries, then human fallback. If it still never lands, it costs you nothing — that's the core guarantee and the ledger proves it.

How do you avoid risky bulk submission?

Submissions are paced per platform (daily per-domain budgets) and staggered across days. You can see the pacing gauge in your portal at any time.

What do I need to start?

Just your domain, a title, a short site summary and a contact email. You verify ownership with a single meta tag — two minutes, no code.

Where do links come from?

A curated pool of 55k+ web directories with per-site reachability intelligence, matched to your niche. English-language markets first.
